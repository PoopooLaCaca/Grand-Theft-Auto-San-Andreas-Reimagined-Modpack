## Why I Made It
Just like many of you, I was incredibly excited when Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition was announced. I followed every bit of news leading up to its release, eager to see these classic games brought back to life. I even pre-ordered it on the very first day it became available on the PlayStation Store.
I was only 14 years old at the time. Now I'm 19, and looking back, I realize just how disappointing those remasters really were. Even then, I knew they weren't the complete experience that longtime fans deserved. To this day, I still don't understand how so much went wrong. In anticipation of the trilogy, Rockstar removed countless community-made mods that had kept these games alive for years. What we received in return was a collection of games that lacked the soul of the originals. The atmosphere felt different, iconic licensed music was removed, textures were often poorly AI-upscaled, and the games launched with bugs that made them difficult to enjoy. Although many of these issues have since been addressed through updates, the first impression—and the trust of many fans—was already lost. That's what inspired me to create this modpack. My goal wasn't to reinvent Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, but to enhance it while staying faithful to Rockstar's original vision. Every mod was carefully selected to feel as though it could have been part of the game from day one. Whether it's visual improvements, quality-of-life features, restored content, or subtle gameplay enhancements, everything has been chosen with the vanilla experience in mind. This isn't meant to be a completely different version of San Andreas. It's meant to feel like the version many of us imagined when we first heard the words "Definitive Edition." Above all else, this project is a love letter to the original game that Rockstar created over twenty years ago and to the incredibly talented modding community whose passion, creativity, and dedication have kept San Andreas alive for generations of players. Without their work, this project would never have been possible. If this modpack helps even one person rediscover why they fell in love with San Andreas all those years ago, then I've accomplished exactly what I set out to do.
